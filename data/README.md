Quote data collected from Hippo.co.za on 9 to 10 October 2026.

`insurance_quotes_clean.xlsx` contains:
- **Quotes:** the raw capture, untouched (150 rows)
- **Clean:** the working sheet with calculated columns (annual premium, price rank, saving vs comprehensive) used by the dashboard
- **Constants:** the fixed variables used for every quote
- **Legend:** what the Status values mean (Quoted, N/A, Not recorded, To verify)
